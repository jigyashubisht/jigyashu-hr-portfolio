Employee Attendance Management System

Excel, Power Query and Power BI

A data-entry sheet records daily attendance for each employee. Formulas build the monthly attendance dashboard, a monthly report with deductions and total salary, and yearly reports from it, and Power Query prepares the data for a Power BI dashboard that shows present, absent and leave days, pay structure and monthly trends by employee.

Tools used

MS ExcelExcel formulasPower QueryPower BI

Key learning outcomes

  • Designing a single data-entry sheet that feeds every report.
  • Cleaning and shaping HR data with Power Query.
  • Turning attendance records into dashboard views HR can act on.
  • Understanding the refresh workflow that carries new entries through to reports.

Salary dashboard, payroll dashboard, attendance input, salary sheet and salary slip

Payroll Management Tracker

MS Excel, advanced formulas and VLOOKUP

An attendance input sheet is linked to monthly and yearly payroll reports and an employee salary sheet. Formulas calculate Metro or Non-Metro classification, HRA, TA, gross salary, PF, ESI and net salary. The project also includes a payroll dashboard, employee salary slips pulled with VLOOKUP, and a leave management component.

Tools used

MS ExcelAdvanced formulasVLOOKUPDashboardsData validation

Key learning outcomes

  • How attendance, allowances and statutory deductions combine into net pay.
  • Building salary slips that retrieve each employee’s own details.
  • Maintaining leave records alongside payroll.
  • Checking formulas carefully, since payroll errors affect real people.
